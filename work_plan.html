ChitChatBusiness Feature Parity Work Plan

Roadmap for closing WhatsApp Business feature gaps

Executive Summary

To achieve parity with WhatsApp Business, we have scoped out and prioritized missing messaging, catalog, and profile features. This work plan splits execution evenly between team members across two core phases.

Phase Focus Area Assigned Owners Priority
Phase 1 Quick Replies & Auto-Messages (Greeting/Away), Chat Labels, Broadcast Lists You & Team Member 2 High
Phase 2 Catalog Cart/Sharing, QR Deep Links, Business Profile Enhancements, Message Analytics You & Team Member 2 Medium

Phase 1: High-Priority Messaging & Core Workflows

Member 1 (You) High Priority
  • Quick Replies System: Build shortcut expansion engine (e.g. typing / prompts saved responses like /greeting).
  • Chat Labels & Tags: Implement color-coded tag management for filtering and organizing customer conversations in the inbox list.
Team Member 2 High Priority
  • Greeting & Away Messages: Implement background logic/auto-responders for first-time inbound messages and scheduled off-hours.
  • Broadcast Lists: Build multi-recipient selection flow to send announcements to contact groups.

Phase 2: Medium-Priority Profile & Commerce Enhancements

Member 1 (You) Medium Priority
  • Catalog Cart & Sharing: Add cart state management and enable sending product cards directly into active chat threads.
  • QR Code / Shareable Link: Create QR generator and deep-link handler for frictionless customer onboarding.
Team Member 2 Medium Priority
  • Enhanced Business Profile: Update Firestore schemas and UI to support industry categories, descriptions, and physical addresses.
  • Message Analytics: Implement basic delivery, sent, and read status counters across threads and broadcasts.
